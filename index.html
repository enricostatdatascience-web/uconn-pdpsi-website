About Us

An Asian-interest fraternity — not Asian-exclusive.

Pi Delta Psi is an Asian American Cultural Fraternity that was founded on February 20, 1994, at the State University of New York at Binghamton. We are an Asian-interest, not Asian-exclusive Fraternity that aims to spread awareness of Asian American culture in an effort to empower the entire Asian American community.

The Omega Chapter was founded on April 12, 2008, at the University of Connecticut — the first Pi Delta Psi chapter in Connecticut and the first Asian-interest fraternity in the state. Our brotherhood continues to grow every semester.

Twelve leaders and friends wanted to make a difference at the University of Connecticut, and took a step toward their goal when they chartered the first Asian-interest fraternity in the state. Omega Chapter History
